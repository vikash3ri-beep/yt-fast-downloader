Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Does this store my downloaded files on the server?

No. The backend operates on an immediate streaming pipeline. Files are temporarily processed and immediately deleted using Python context generators after the HTTP chunk transfer finishes.

Can I download YouTube Shorts and high-resolution videos?

Yes! Both YouTube Shorts (youtube.com/shorts/...) and full-length videos are supported with 1080p, 720p, 480p, and 360p video options.

Why did my download fail on a specific video?

Videos that are marked Private, live broadcasts in progress, or content requiring account age-verification cannot be downloaded without authenticated credentials.
